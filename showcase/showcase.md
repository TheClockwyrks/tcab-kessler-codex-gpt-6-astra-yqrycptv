# Kessler

The sky is full of dead metal. You have one deflector, three lives, and a planet to keep safe. Circle low orbit and send a spinning demolition ball into three nested rings of derelict machinery.

Every impact opens a new route. The inner ring holds steady while the outer rings turn against each other; armored targets need a second hit. Meet the ball near the edge of your deflector to steer its next climb through the debris. Miss it, and watch it burn against the planet below.

Wreckage brings opportunity. Catch falling salvage to widen your deflector, raise a one-use shield, split the attack into several balls, or punch straight through armor. Read the pod before you commit: the amber chevrons shrink your safety margin.

Clear the whole orbit for a wave bonus, then take on a faster field. Keep the cascade going and see how much sky you can reclaim.
